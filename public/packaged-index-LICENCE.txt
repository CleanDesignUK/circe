CIRCE selected a subset of products listed for sale in Indonesia or the United Kingdom. This country tag describes a market and does not establish the country of manufacture. Each row is identified by its source barcode; distinct barcodes can refer to different pack sizes or market formulations. Names and brands reflect the contributor records. Energy is in kcal; protein, carbohydrate and fat are in grams per 100 g or 100 ml as recorded by the source. Energy in kJ was divided by 4.184 when no kcal field was available. A numeric serving appears only when the record supplied an explicit gram or millilitre portion.
